Lecso

Still one of my go-to weeknight dinners

Did I mention that I never learned how to cook at home? I started intentionally learning it in my twenties, when I was a student and moved in with my boyfriend at the time. Back then, there was a national newspaper that published recipes once a week. Once a month, they also featured a special “step-by-step” recipe, with detailed instructions for preparing a particular dish.

That’s how I learned to make my first proper meals! One of those step-by-step recipes was this Hungarian-inspired lecsó, which I still make regularly to this day. In the meantime, I became a pescatarian, so I now use the best vegan sausages I can find. And honestly, the result is awesome.

Ingredients for 2 servings

  • 4 tomatoes
  • 4 bell peppers
  • 1 onion
  • Vegan sausages
  • Fresh parsley for serving

Steps

  1. Slice the onion and fry it in a little oil.
  2. Add the tomatoes, cut into small cubes. There’s no need to peel them.
  3. Add the bell peppers, sliced into strips. Cover and let everything simmer for about 15 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
  4. Meanwhile, slice the vegan sausages and fry them separately until lightly browned.
  5. Serve the lecso with the fried sausages and sprinkle with fresh parsley.

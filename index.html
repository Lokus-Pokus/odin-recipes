Odin Recipes

Here are some of the very first recipes I ever learned to make, back when I was a teenager. My parents didn’t cook, so my brother and I mostly survived on school canteen food. My journey into cooking was a long and sometimes bumpy one, but somewhere along the way, I learned to really enjoy it.

These days, I sometimes surprise myself when I’m eating out. I’ll taste a dish in a restaurant and realise that I could probably make something just as good at home—or, every now and then, even better. These recipes are a little snapshot of that journey: the dishes that taught me how to cook and that have stayed with me over the years.
